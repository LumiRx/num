## picks
Fontana di Trevi :: Go at seven in the morning or after midnight. Any other hour you are looking at the back of a phone, and the piazza is small enough that this genuinely decides whether the visit was worth it.
Fontana dei Quattro Fiumi :: Bernini's, in the middle of Piazza Navona, with the four river gods. The one to sit with rather than circle — take a seat on the north side where the afternoon light hits the obelisk.
Fontana Dell'Acqua Paola :: Up on the Janiculum, and the reason to climb is not the fountain but what is behind you when you turn around: the whole city, laid flat, for free.
Fontana dei Libri :: A small wall fountain on Via degli Staderari, carved with books and a stag's head for the old university quarter. Nobody queues for it and it is thirty seconds off a street you were walking anyway.
Fontana della Botte :: In Trastevere, shaped like a wine barrel with two spouts, a nod to the carts that brought wine down from the Castelli. Local, small, and the sort of thing you only find if somebody points at it.

## know
Rome has roughly 2,500 *nasoni* — cast-iron street taps with a curved spout, running cold, drinkable, free, all day and all night. That is the fountain fact that actually changes a day in August: you do not need to buy water in Rome, ever. Block the end of the spout with a finger and a jet arcs up through the small hole on top, which is how Romans drink from them without touching anything. The ten fountains listed here are the monuments; the nasoni are the plumbing, and the plumbing is what you will use.

## faq
How many fountains are there in Rome? :: NUM's directory maps ten named monumental fountains in Rome with coordinates. The city also has around 2,500 *nasoni* — the small cast-iron street taps that run free drinking water day and night — which are not counted as monuments here.
Can you drink the water from Rome's fountains? :: From the *nasoni*, the small cast-iron street taps, yes — it is mains drinking water, cold and free. Not from the monumental fountains such as the Trevi, which recirculate.
What time should you visit the Trevi Fountain? :: Around seven in the morning, or after midnight. The piazza is small and at any other hour the view is of other people's phones.
