# Film 2 — "Table Tonight"

**30 seconds · 9:16 vertical · 4K (2160×3840) · Higgsfield + nano banana pro**
Written 2026-08-05. Companion to Film 1 (`NUM_Film1_YT_Shorts`).

---

## The one line

*You land somewhere loud, you text one sentence, and a stranger who runs the
room says yes.*

Not "an app for nightlife." The film is about the **three seconds between
asking and being expected somewhere** — which is the only thing Num actually
sells.

## Why this beat and not a party montage

Anyone can cut a club reel. What nobody else can show is the **venue's side**:
a host's phone lighting up, a thumb tapping CONFIRM, and the guest's screen
changing while they're still in the taxi. That shot is the product. Everything
before it is setup and everything after it is payoff.

Reference is Expedia's *Going Places with Ken* — the craft to borrow is
restraint: real faces, unhurried camera, one arc. The energy is ours.

## ⚠️ Claims gates — read before rendering

| Rule | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **No payment through Num** | Never show a card, a total, or "paid". Guests pay the venue directly (Terms §3). Bottle appears on the table, never on a receipt. |
| **Confirmed, not guaranteed** | The venue confirms. Num relays. On-screen word is **CONFIRMED**, never "GUARANTEED" or "VIP access". |
| **No "best" / "top" / counts** | `top_places` is not quality-ranked and the published place-count is known-wrong. No numbers on screen except the time and the party size. |
| **Fictional venue** | Use **SALT** — invented. Never name a real club; it implies a partnership that doesn't exist. |
| **No real people** | No lookalikes of public figures. |
| **§8 imagery exception** | AI-generated, under the exception Dre granted 2026-08-05 (see `ads/reddit/CAMPAIGN.md`). |
| **Captions burned in** | Reddit, TikTok and Shorts autoplay muted. Every line of meaning must be legible with sound off. |

## Cutting notes

- **The hard cut at 0:13** is the film. Music stops, room tone only. Everything
  before it is a phone; everything after it is a person. Do not smooth it.
- **Hold 0:21** a beat longer than feels comfortable. The relief is the sell.
- **Never show the club before 0:22.** Showing the payoff early turns it into a
  nightlife ad, and nightlife ads are interchangeable.
- **0:29 caption is "Text it. It's handled."** — not "Book now", not "Download".
  It matches what the product does and how a person would say it.
