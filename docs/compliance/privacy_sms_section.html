5 · Text messages (SMS)

Where we offer NUM by SMS text message, this section applies in addition to the rest of this policy. It describes what we send, how you consent, and how you stop.

5.1 What we send

Customer-care and transactional messages only: replies to what you ask your concierge, and updates about arrangements you asked us to make — a table, a driver, a tour, a change to a booking. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages, and we do not send third-party content. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

5.2 How you opt in

You give consent by entering your own mobile number on our sign-up page and ticking the consent box — it is never pre-ticked, never bundled with anything else, and consent is not a condition of any purchase. You also opt in by texting our number first. We never message a number we did not receive direct consent from, and we never buy, rent, or import phone lists.

5.3 How you stop or get help

Reply STOP (or END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT) at any time. We send one confirmation and nothing after that — immediately and permanently. Reply HELP for support contact details, or reach us any time at info@5arz.com.

5.4 Mobile information is never shared for marketing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors who provide support services for the messaging program — such as our cloud, AI, and messaging infrastructure providers — is permitted so they can deliver the service on our behalf under contract. All other use-case categories exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5.5 What we keep

Your mobile number, your consent record (when and how you opted in), and the messages exchanged with your concierge, handled as described elsewhere in this policy. Text FORGET to erase your preference profile instantly, or contact us to request deletion of your data. Opt-out records are retained so we can honor your opt-out permanently.
