# NUM by 5arz — Partner Demo Script (15 min, two phones)

**Setup before the meeting:** `mobile_prototype.html` open full-screen on YOUR phone (add to home screen — it launches like an app). Deck on the room screen. If deployed by meeting day: second phone with real WhatsApp sandbox for the live text. **Fallback:** the console's Live-conversations feed shows real transcripts — demo works fully offline.

**The one sentence:** *"Every passenger in your cars gets a personal AI concierge in their own language — it books, it remembers, it finds the whales — and everyone in your operation runs it from their phone."*

---

## Beat 1 — The user (3 min) · "watch a tourist use it"

1. Show a QR card: "This lives on your seatback screens. Scan → opens WhatsApp/LINE/WeChat — no app install, nothing to download."
2. **If live:** have THEM text สวัสดี or 你好 to the sandbox number → reply lands in their language in ~2s, with the PDPA notice. **If not live:** open console → Operator → Today → tap the Thai LINE conversation — walk the transcript: user asks for seafood in Chalong → *the AI searches only YOUR merchant catalogue* → recommends Kan Eang @ Pier → offers to book.
3. Point at the tool line in the transcript: "It can't invent restaurants. It only recommends businesses we've approved together. Your network IS the product."
4. Tap the Chinese WHALE conversation: "Watch what happens when someone mentions buying a villa — it qualifies budget and timeline in Chinese, then hands it to a human. Your human."

## Beat 2 — The whale engine (4 min) · "where the money is"

5. Switch role → **Master → Whales**. The red SLA-breach card first: "Every high-value lead has a clock. Nothing rots in an inbox."
6. Tap a lead → the detail sheet: budget band, timeline, party, **What NUM knows** (memory), suggested next touch. "Your specialist opens this and already knows the family, the school constraint, the budget — the AI briefed them."
7. Tap **Assign** → routed to the RE specialist + Slack ping. "30/70 in your favor on these. The pilot target is thirty of these in month one." (June model: ~30 leads ≈ $9k, your share ~$6.3k of it.)

## Beat 3 — The business side (3 min) · "your merchants manage themselves"

8. Role → **Merchant**. This Week: bookings, revenue, NPS. Bookings tab → tap **Accept** on the Chinese family of 5. "The restaurant confirms from their phone in one tap. No calls, no email."
9. Listing tab: "The merchant edits photos, hours, promos — and the exact words the AI uses to sell them. The 'Asks' tab shows them anonymized demand: six people asked about spice levels this week."
10. Role → **Master → Supply** → approval queue: "New merchants go live to the AI only when approved — that's 5arz Verify. Quality stays high because nothing unvetted ever gets recommended."

## Beat 4 — The command deck (3 min) · "you see everything"

11. **Master → Flow**: "Today's whole circuit on one screen — scans to chats to AI actions to bookings to baht. Four conversion rates; when one drops, we both know exactly where to look."
12. **Traffic**: "Every car ranked. Car 017 on the airport–Patong run: 34 scans, 47% conversion. Car 009: two scans — screen placement problem, fixable tomorrow. Your fleet becomes a measurable media network."
13. **Executive** role, Scorecard: "This is our weekly meeting — the 7 pilot KPIs against targets, live. The Week-7 gate isn't a debate, it's a dashboard."
14. Tap the **EN⇄TH** toggle somewhere along the way — the whole console flips to Thai. Always gets a reaction.

## Close (2 min)

"The system is built — 84 automated tests, security clean, PDPA-compliant with real data erasure. What turns it on is: your Meta Business for the WhatsApp sender, the WeChat account decision, the first 40 merchants with your intros, and QRs in 30–40 cars. Tech is ready this week. The month starts when the cars do."

**The five decisions to leave with:** vehicle count (30/40) · WeChat SA (yours vs fresh) · concierge handler staffing · merchant intro list owner · pilot start date.

*Pro tips: keep the phone in dark mode + full brightness · pre-set role to Master/Flow before handing the phone over · let THEM tap Accept and Assign — the person who presses the button believes the product.*
