## What changed and why

**Responses**
- `answercache.mjs` — cache hits now carry `picks` (a hit used to say "three
  near you, the first is what I'd do" over zero cards); key includes a ~1 km
  cell; "near me" questions are not cached without a position; DDL once per
  isolate. `index.mjs` passes the position at both ends.
- `quality.mjs` — `deflected-with-context` fires only when the block holds
  verified content (it fired on the 850-char EMPTY block and then told the
  model to "answer from the verified block above"); `figuresIn` knows the
  currencies of the 38 countries we are live in, not four.
- `specialists.mjs`, `prompt.mjs`, `brains.mjs` — one length cap (3 sentences /
  40 words, the schema's), one option rule (three for places, one for
  decisions), no separate acknowledgement line, no generic "anything else?",
  no example that models a commitment Num can't make, no "honest fare band".
- `redact.mjs` — ISO dates no longer match the phone pattern, so
  `trip_dates` reaches the model and stops being re-asked.

**Storage**
- `health.mjs` — `checkStorage` reads `meta.size_after` instead of
  `COUNT(*) FROM places` every five minutes (774M rows/day, ~$23/mo).
- `ai/places.js` + 0016 — `isKnownArea` and `areaCenter` read the 16.5K-row
  `num_dest_areas` table; unsupported-city asks drop from 940 ms to a seek.
- `retention.mjs` (new) + 0017 — the sweep that makes `num_retention_policy`
  true; hourly from `scheduled()`.
- `account.mjs` — deletion targets `num_dms` (was `num_dm`, silently failing,
  so DMs survived account deletion), drops the nonexistent `num_prefs`, and
  wipes nine personal tables it missed. Financial tables deliberately kept.

**Connections / measurement**
- `asks.mjs` — probe questions are marked `synthetic=1` at write time and
  `NOT_PROBE` excludes them in `health`, `console`, `bizdash`
  ("what travellers ask for near you" was showing our uptime probe ×217),
  `bizagent`. They were 61% of rows and 46% of `degraded=1`.
- `index.mjs` — `feature_requests.asked` is scrubbed before it is stored
  (row 5 held a raw phone number; scrubbed in place on 4 Sep).

## Reviewed and left alone
- `social.mjs` `b.verify !== false` — flagged by the audit as an OTP bypass.
  It is not: the flag is honoured only for a NEW number with no account to
  take over, the client uses it for name/avatar updates, and SEC-006 × SEC-001
  already closed the takeover path. Documented at social.mjs:455-500.
