What NUM does

One platform, built end-to-end.

Every capability below is running code today — not a roadmap.

💬

Four channels, one brain

WhatsApp, LINE, WeChat, and SMS through a single AI. The same memory and tools everywhere the traveler already is.

🌏

Nine languages, auto-detected

Replies in the traveler's own language from the first message — Chinese buyers qualify in Chinese, Russians in Russian. No funnel drop at the language barrier.

🧠

Persistent memory

NUM remembers each person across weeks and channels — diet, family, trip dates, budget. Day 30 knows what day 2 learned. That continuity is the product.

🐋

The whale-lead engine

Detects high-value intent — property, schools, relocation, long-stay visas — qualifies budget and timeline, and hands a briefed lead to your specialist with an SLA timer.

🏪

Merchant self-serve

Local businesses manage bookings, hours, photos, and the exact words NUM uses to sell them — from their own phone. Anonymized guest demand comes back as free market research.

🔒

Never invents

NUM recommends only businesses you've approved. It cannot fabricate a restaurant, a price, or a review. Your network is the product; quality is the moat.

📍

Per-vehicle attribution

Every car and hotel QR is tracked, so your fleet becomes a measurable media network — you see which routes convert and which need a placement fix.

🛡️

Compliant by design

PDPA consent shown on first contact, audited; "delete" in any language triggers real data erasure; PII scrubbed before storage. Trust is what makes it bookable.

📊

Every message measured

Per-conversation cost, language, intent, and outcome logged in real time. Four conversions — scan, chat, action, revenue — on one screen.
