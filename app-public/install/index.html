Put Num on your home screen

It's a web app, so there's nothing to download from a store and no account to create. Installing means it opens full-screen, keeps your thread, and can notify you when a booking is confirmed.

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome — only Safari can install on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, top right.
  2. Choose Install app — or Add to Home screen if you don't see it.
  3. Confirm. Num opens like any other app.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before they start

Is it free?

Yes. The free tier is the whole concierge, not a demo of one — asking, planning and booking all work without paying us anything. There are paid tiers for people who want more, but you will never be told "upgrade to ask a question". If you book something that costs money, you pay the venue as you normally would.

Do I have to download an app?

No. Num runs in your browser and you can start right now. Adding it to your home screen takes about ten seconds and makes it behave like a normal app — full screen, your thread kept, and notifications when a booking is confirmed. There's no App Store or Play Store step today.

Do you cover the city I'm going to?

77 destinations are live right now and the directory holds over 2.5 million places, with the deepest coverage across Thailand. If you ask about somewhere we don't cover properly, Num tells you so plainly instead of inventing a recommendation — that's a deliberate rule, not a limitation we're hiding.

Do I need an account?

No. Open it and start typing. You only give a phone number or email if you want your thread on more than one device, or want us to reach you about a booking.

What do you do with what I tell it?

Num keeps a short profile of your preferences so it doesn't ask twice — things like "vegetarian", "travelling with kids". It deliberately does not store health conditions, religion, politics, ID or payment details, even if you mention them. Text FORGET at any time and it's erased. Profiles of people who haven't used it in 12 months are deleted automatically. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Is it a bot, or real people?

Both, honestly. The concierge answers you directly, and real bookings go to real venues who confirm them. If something can't be arranged, you're told — you won't be left with a confirmation that reaches nobody.

What languages does it speak?

English and Thai today, and it replies in whichever you write in.

Who's behind it?

Num is built by 5arz. If you want a human, you can reach us any time from inside the thread — just ask.
