The six tools, and what each one refuses
  • find_listing — find your place by name or destination. No key needed; every other tool needs the place_id it returns.
  • start_claim — NUM sends a code to the phone or email already published on the listing. Your assistant cannot choose where it goes — that is what makes it proof.
  • complete_claim — trade the code for an API key. Shown once, never recoverable.
  • get_listing — read what NUM currently says about you.
  • update_listing — change name, phone, website, hours, cuisine, address. Category, rating and where you rank are not editable, and no key grants it.
  • get_insights — how often NUM surfaced you to a guest.
  • list_offerings — read what you offer and what you charge: menu, treatments, rooms or tours. A price can be absent — “market price” is a real answer and comes back as a note rather than a number.
  • set_offering — add or change one item. Currency is not settable: it comes from where your business is, so a Thai restaurant is never priced in dollars. Leave the price empty when it varies.
  • hide_offering — stop NUM mentioning something seasonal or sold out, without deleting it. It comes back without retyping.

The machine-readable index is at /api/biz. If this list and that endpoint disagree, the endpoint is right and this page is stale — tell us at info@itsnum.com.

Business listing FAQ

What does it cost?

Listing is free. 10% of the bill on completed bookings only — or $2 per confirmed table if nothing you use reports the bill. Never both.

How fast can I be live?

As soon as verification clears. It is a human check, which is what makes the verified mark mean something.

Who can claim my listing?

Only someone who can receive a code at the contact already published on it. That is why nobody else can take your place.

Can an AI assistant run this for me?

Yes — over our API and MCP server, free on every plan. A human at 5arz approves anything an agent submits before travellers see it.
