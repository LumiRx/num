Which one you want

  • You run a business and want to be listed. Start at itsnum.com/claim — it is faster than emailing, because most places are already in the directory and only need claiming.
  • You are already listed and something is wrong. Email info@itsnum.com with the business name and the city. Include the booking reference if it is about a booking.
  • You are a traveller and a booking went wrong. Message Num on LINE in the same thread as the booking — it has the context and can fix it faster.
  • You build AI agents. See itsnum.com/agents. It has the API, the MCP endpoint and the keys, and you do not need to talk to anyone to start.
  • Press, partnerships, or you want NUM in your city. info@itsnum.com, and say which.

Removing a listing

If a business has been listed and does not want to be, email info@itsnum.com from an address at that business's domain, or call the number above. It comes down; we do not make you argue for it.
