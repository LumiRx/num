Official Rules

Last updated 13 September 2026.

Sponsor

5arz Inc., operator of Num (itsnum.com).

No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win, and no payment will improve your chance of winning. A purchase does not increase your odds.

Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States and the United Kingdom who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Employees of 5arz Inc., their immediate families and members of their households are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Thailand is not currently included — see rule 11.

How to enter

There are two free methods, and they carry equal weight:

(a) By text. Send the single word PACKS to +1 424 346 0888 during the entry period. Standard message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of entry or of winning.

(b) By using Num. Hold a registered Num account and send Num at least one message during the entry period. You are entered automatically, with nothing further to do.

One entry per person per entry period, regardless of how many times you text or how many messages you send. Entering by both methods does not give you two entries.

Entry period

Each weekly entry period begins Friday at 00:00 UTC and closes the following Thursday at 23:59 UTC. Draws take place each Friday. Entries received outside a period are held for the next one.

Prize

Ten (10) winners per entry period. Each winner receives one (1) sealed Pokémon trading card pack. Approximate retail value US$4–8 each; total approximate retail value per week US$40–80. Prizes are genuine sealed product bought at retail by the Sponsor. Prizes are not transferable and no cash alternative is offered except at the Sponsor's sole discretion.

Odds

Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received in that entry period. The Sponsor publishes the eligible count with each week's result.

Winner selection

Winners are drawn at random from all eligible entries by an automated process. The random seed is recorded before the winners are computed from it, together with the eligible count and the result, so that any draw can be reproduced and checked afterwards.

Notification and claim

Winners are notified in the Num app, and by text where they have entered by text, within 48 hours of the draw. A winner must supply a shipping address within 14 days. Unclaimed prizes are redrawn. The Sponsor may require confirmation of age and residence before shipping.

Shipping

Free, to addresses in the United States and the United Kingdom only.

Thailand

Thai residents are not currently eligible for the draw, pending a licence under the Thai Gambling Act. Thai members receive a separate, guaranteed benefit instead, which is not a prize draw and requires no licence.

Your data

Entry data is limited to your phone number, whether you entered, and your existing use of Num. A winner's shipping address is used only to send that prize and is deleted afterwards. Texting PACKS records your consent to receive messages from Num; you can withdraw it at any time by replying STOP, and doing so does not remove an entry you have already made.

Publicity

Winners may be announced by first name and city only. No full name, handle, phone number or address is ever published. A winner may decline to be named and still receive the prize.

Changes

The Sponsor may suspend, cancel or amend the draw where it is necessary to do so, including for legal or technical reasons. Any change is published on this page with an updated date. Entries already made for the current period are honoured.
