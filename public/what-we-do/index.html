For AI agents

What the 10% actually means

Most platforms charge you for being seen. We charge for a result, and only that result.

  • Nothing on walk-ins. Someone who found you on their own is yours.
  • Nothing on your own repeat customers. A guest who comes back directly is yours too.
  • Nothing on a no-show. A booking that does not happen costs you nothing.
  • No monthly minimum, no listing fee, no ad spend.
  • Never added to the guest’s bill. It comes out of your side, so your prices stay your prices.

Optional dashboards are separate and genuinely optional — the listing stays live whether or not you take one. Full pricing.

One word we use precisely

“Verified” on NUM describes a person or a claimed listing — never a place. A verified guest is a traveler confirmed to be a unique real human. A verified listing is one whose owner proved they control the contact details already published on it. Unclaimed listings are mapped, not verified.

It is not a review score, and we will not let it drift into one. The full specification — what we check, how, and what we deliberately refuse to call verified — is on the verification page.
