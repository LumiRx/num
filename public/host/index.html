Your shelf

Products

Three kinds, one shelf. Your own products — hampers, packaged experiences, anything with a fixed price. NUM's products you can put in front of your clients. And Ghost Message lines: a code a client texts to get something delivered.

The Ghost rule. A Ghost line needs a SKU, a keyword, a photo and a real price before it can go live. A code that resolves to nothing is worse than no code at all — the client texted it off a card and got silence. Save an incomplete line and we keep it as a draft rather than pretending it works.

NUM keeps booking their travel directly, as it does for any member without a host — and it is not charged to them either, because NUM never bills a traveller.

Leaving NUM

Close your host account

Everyone in your book is released and told directly — they are told it was an account closure, not anything about them. Your plan is cancelled, you are not billed again, your coverage and your network connections end, and this console link stops working.

We keep the record of the bookings you confirmed and of each release, because that is what makes a later question answerable. We do not keep working on anything for you. Your clients’ details are yours — ask before you close and we will send you the list.

Not a checkbox. A checkbox is one tap away from a scroll, and this ends relationships that belong to other people.
