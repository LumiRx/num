How it works

Two sides. Neither one is complicated.

For your guest

1

They scan the code

Camera or a tap. Every code is NFC and QR on the same face, so it works on any phone.

2

Their tab opens

Venue, table, running total, and everyone else sitting with them. No app needed to join.

3

They pay their part

All of it, an even split, or whatever they choose. PromptPay or card, on their own phone.

For you

1

Stick the codes on

Label them in your console once. Table 1 to 8, the bar, the terrace. Takes a few minutes.

2

Type the total

That is the whole operation. No POS to connect, no terminal to buy, no float to count.

3

Get paid

Your phone tells you the moment a table settles. The money follows the same night.

Group pay

Everyone at the table scans the same code. The tab knows who is on it, what is left and who has already paid. Nobody works out a share on a napkin, and nobody chases anyone afterwards.
