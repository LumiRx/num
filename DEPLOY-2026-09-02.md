## Then test it the way a stranger will

Not on desktop. From Instagram, on a phone:

1. Put `itsnum.com/app` in a story or your bio and tap it **from inside the
   Instagram app** — the in-app browser is the whole point, and opening it in
   Safari tests nothing.
2. You should see **"Num works in Instagram's browser"** and a green
   **Start using Num** button. The old "Put Num on your home screen" hero
   should be gone.
3. Tap **Start using Num**. The app should load and let you type — with **no
   card interrupting you**.
4. Ask it something real. Only *after* your first message should the
   home-screen card appear, and it should say your account travels with your
   number — never that anything may be lost.
5. If anything goes black, you now get **"Num stopped short"** with a Reload
   button instead of a dead screen. Tap Reload; it clears the cached bundle
   first.

If step 5 ever fires, it lands in analytics as `app_crash` with the message
and the user-agent. That is the thing we did not have last night — the black
screen left no record at all, which is why it was a guess.

# 3 Sep — the home page, put back

## What happened

On 1 Sep a separate session rewrote `public/index.html` into the ads landing
page (ask box, pixel, no navigation). Its deploy notes listed the app and
growth workers only — **not `wrangler.jsonc`**, the console worker that
serves `public/`. So the rewrite sat on disk, never live.

On 2 Sep I asked for `npx wrangler deploy --config wrangler.jsonc` to ship
the Instagram in-app-browser fix for `/app/`. That deploy carries **all** of
`public/`, and it carried the home-page rewrite with it. I did not check what
else in `public/` was undeployed before asking for that command. That is how
the front door changed without anyone asking for it.

Confirm it, if you want the receipt:
```
npx wrangler deployments list --config wrangler.jsonc
```
If the deployment before yesterday's predates 1 Sep 21:13, that is the one.

## Two things only you can settle

1. **Is the 4 Aug page the "old website" you mean?** If there was a newer
   version between 4 Aug and 1 Sep, it is in git:
   `git log --oneline -6 -- public/index.html` and
   `git checkout <sha> -- public/index.html`.
2. **The WhatsApp number.** There is no live WhatsApp sender (`worker/whatsapp.mjs`

   button is wired to a `wa.me` link and hidden until `WA_NUMBER` in
   `public/ask/index.html` holds a real number — digits, country code first.
   A visible button that opens a dead chat is worse than none.
